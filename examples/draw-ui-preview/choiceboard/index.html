What is Would You Rather?

Would You Rather Questions power a social choice game: players face two alternatives and must choose one. There are no mathematically correct answers. The fun comes from the trade-off—what matters most, what feels funny, and how everyone defends their instinct.

From clean questions for kids to tough dilemmas for friends and couples, the format cuts through small talk. It works around a dinner table, in a classroom, on a road trip, or as a quick icebreaker.

Can I use Presenter Mode?

The current interface includes a fullscreen presenter view with keyboard navigation for classrooms, projectors, and shared screens. This preview shows its visual treatment.

Can I filter questions by audience or occasion?

The product has filters for age group, relationship, occasion, tone, and difficulty. The current source snapshot marks approved questions as playable.

Can I see community voting results?

The voting flow is designed to reveal A/B percentages and totals after a vote. Any numbers in this prototype are marked as illustrative and are not live results.
